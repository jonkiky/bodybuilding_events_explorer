
# Major USA Bodybuilding Organizations

This document lists the most prominent bodybuilding organizations in the United States, including both mainstream and natural federations.

---

## 🏆 Major USA Bodybuilding Organizations
*(Sorted by approximate number of competitions hosted annually in the USA)*

### 1. **National Physique Committee (NPC)**
- **Description**: The largest amateur bodybuilding organization in the U.S., and the official feeder league into the IFBB Pro League. Founded in 1982 by Jim Manion, the NPC hosts over 200 contests annually across all 50 states. Divisions include bodybuilding, classic physique, men's physique, bikini, figure, wellness, and fit body. Winners of overall titles at national-level NPC shows earn IFBB Pro League status. The organization is known for its large competitor fields, professional production quality, and serving as the primary pathway to professional bodybuilding careers.
- **Annual Competitions**: 200+ contests across all 50 states
- **Influence**: Extremely High - Largest amateur organization, direct pathway to IFBB Pro League, shapes industry standards
- **Key Features**: No drug testing, multiple divisions, regional and national qualifiers, direct path to IFBB Pro status
- **Annual Events**: NPC Nationals, USA Championships, Teen/Collegiate/Masters Nationals
- **Website**: [npcnewsonline.com](https://npcnewsonline.com)

### 2. **Organization of Competitive Bodybuilders (OCB)**
- **Description**: Natural federation with over 25 years of drug-tested competitions, known for family-friendly atmosphere and comprehensive novice divisions. Founded in 1999, the OCB has established itself as one of the most welcoming natural bodybuilding organizations for beginners and experienced competitors alike. The federation emphasizes creating a supportive environment where athletes can develop their skills and advance through clearly defined competitive levels.
- **Annual Competitions**: 80-100+ contests nationwide
- **Influence**: High - Leading natural federation, beginner-friendly approach, strong community presence
- **Key Features**: Drug testing, beginner-friendly divisions, family atmosphere, comprehensive novice categories
- **Testing Protocol**: Random urinalysis, banned substance education, fair appeals process
- **Divisions**: Natural bodybuilding, figure, bikini, physique, fitness, various novice categories
- **Website**: [ocbonline.com](https://ocbonline.com)

### 3. **National Gym Association (NGA)**
- **Description**: Founded in 1979, non-profit organization with national natural competitions and both pro and amateur divisions. One of the oldest natural bodybuilding organizations in the United States, the NGA has been promoting drug-free competition for over four decades. The organization operates as a true non-profit with a mission to provide affordable, fair, and drug-tested competitions for athletes nationwide. NGA offers both amateur and professional divisions, with clear advancement pathways and maintains a strong emphasis on community building and athlete mentorship.
- **Annual Competitions**: 60-80 contests across multiple regions
- **Influence**: High - Pioneer in natural bodybuilding, 40+ year legacy, non-profit community focus
- **Key Features**: Non-profit status, 40+ year history, pro and amateur divisions, affordable competition fees, community focus
- **Testing Standards**: Comprehensive drug testing, prohibited substance education, fair appeals process
- **Pro Divisions**: Natural bodybuilding, figure, bikini, fitness, physique
- **Community Programs**: Youth outreach, educational seminars, training workshops
- **Website**: [nationalgym.com](https://www.nationalgym.com)

### 4. **United States Bodybuilding Federation (USBF)**
- **Description**: Promotes natural bodybuilding and drug-tested competitions across the country. Established as a grassroots organization dedicated to providing drug-free competitive opportunities for athletes of all levels. The USBF hosts regional and national championships with comprehensive drug testing protocols and emphasizes sportsmanship, fair play, and athlete development. The federation is known for its supportive community atmosphere and commitment to helping natural athletes achieve their competitive goals through proper training, nutrition, and contest preparation guidance.
- **Annual Competitions**: 40-60 regional and national contests
- **Influence**: Moderate-High - Grassroots natural organization, athlete development focus, regional stronghold
- **Key Features**: Drug testing at all levels, beginner-friendly atmosphere, comprehensive athlete support, affordable competition fees
- **Testing Protocol**: Random and scheduled urinalysis, banned substance education, appeals process
- **Support Services**: Contest prep seminars, posing clinics, nutrition guidance, mentorship programs
- **Website**: [usbf.net](https://usbf.net) or [usbfbodybuilding.com](https://www.usbfbodybuilding.com)

### 5. **IFBB Professional League (IFBB Pro League)**
- **Description**: The premier professional bodybuilding league that sanctions Mr. Olympia, Arnold Classic, and more. Works alongside NPC for amateur qualification. Established in 1946 by Joe and Ben Weider, the IFBB Pro League represents the pinnacle of competitive bodybuilding. Professional athletes compete for the most prestigious titles in the sport, including Mr. Olympia (the ultimate bodybuilding title), Ms. Olympia, and the Arnold Classic. The league oversees all major professional contests worldwide and maintains the highest standards of competition production and athlete presentation.
- **Annual Competitions**: 30-40 professional contests in the USA
- **Influence**: Extremely High - Premier professional league, global recognition, industry pinnacle
- **Key Features**: Professional-only competitions, highest prize money, worldwide recognition, elite athlete roster
- **Major Events**: Mr. Olympia, Arnold Classic, New York Pro, Tampa Pro, Chicago Pro
- **Prize Money**: Multi-million dollar total annual payouts across all divisions
- **Website**: [ifbbpro.com](https://ifbbpro.com)

### 6. **North American Natural Bodybuilding Federation (NANBF)**
- **Description**: Established natural organization with strict 7-year drug-free requirements and polygraph testing protocols. The NANBF maintains some of the most rigorous drug-free standards in natural bodybuilding, requiring competitors to demonstrate extended periods of drug-free training and undergo comprehensive testing including polygraph examinations.
- **Annual Competitions**: 25-35 contests across North America
- **Influence**: Moderate-High - Rigorous testing standards, North American presence, credibility leader
- **Key Features**: 7-year drug-free requirement, polygraph testing, strict eligibility standards
- **Testing Protocol**: Polygraph examination, urinalysis, extensive drug-free history verification
- **Regional Focus**: Strong presence across North America with regional and national championships
- **Website**: [nanbf.net](https://nanbf.net)

### 7. **American Natural Bodybuilding Federation (ANBF)**
- **Description**: Founded in 2013, WADA-aligned drug-free competitions requiring 5 years of drug-free eligibility. The ANBF follows World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA) protocols and maintains one of the most comprehensive drug testing programs in natural bodybuilding. Competitors must provide detailed training and supplement histories, undergo both scheduled and random drug testing, and demonstrate a minimum 5-year commitment to drug-free training. The federation emphasizes fair competition, athlete safety, and maintaining the integrity of natural bodybuilding.
- **Annual Competitions**: 20-30 WADA-compliant contests
- **Influence**: Moderate - WADA compliance leader, scientific approach, growing reputation
- **Key Features**: WADA-compliant testing, 5-year drug-free requirement, random testing protocols, comprehensive banned substance list
- **Testing Standards**: WADA-approved laboratories, blood and urine analysis, year-round testing availability
- **Divisions**: Natural bodybuilding, classic physique, men's physique, bikini, figure, fitness
- **Website**: [anbfnatural.com](https://anbfnatural.com)

### 8. **Southern Natural Bodybuilding Federation (SNBF)**
- **Description**: Regional natural organization serving the Southern United States. The SNBF focuses on providing high-quality natural bodybuilding competitions throughout the southeastern region, emphasizing community building and athlete development within the natural bodybuilding culture.
- **Annual Competitions**: 15-25 regional contests in the Southern USA
- **Influence**: Moderate - Strong regional presence, Southern USA focus, community building
- **Key Features**: Regional focus, community-oriented, natural competition emphasis
- **Geographic Scope**: Primarily serves Southern and Southeastern United States
- **Community Focus**: Strong emphasis on local athlete development and support

### 9. **World Natural Bodybuilding Federation (WNBF)**
- **Description**: Elite natural federation with strict drug testing including polygraphs and long-term drug-free eligibility. Founded in 1989, the WNBF is considered the gold standard for natural bodybuilding competitions. Athletes must pass rigorous screening including urinalysis, polygraph examinations, and demonstrate 5+ years of drug-free training history. The organization maintains a lifetime ban policy for any positive drug test and requires all competitors to sign legal affidavits under penalty of perjury regarding their drug-free status.
- **Annual Competitions**: 10-20 elite-level competitions (including international)
- **Influence**: High - Gold standard for natural bodybuilding, elite competition level, industry credibility
- **Key Features**: Polygraph testing, lifetime drug-free requirement, scientific judging criteria, international recognition
- **Testing Protocol**: Urine analysis, polygraph examination, 5-year drug-free affidavit
- **Notable Events**: WNBF World Championships, Pro/Am World Championships
- **Website**: [worldnaturalbb.com](https://worldnaturalbb.com)

### 10. **Professional Natural Bodybuilding Association (PNBA)**
- **Description**: Long-established natural federation with professional and amateur divisions. The PNBA has been promoting natural bodybuilding competition for decades, offering pathways for amateur athletes to advance to professional status while maintaining strict drug-free standards throughout all levels of competition.
- **Annual Competitions**: 8-15 professional and amateur contests
- **Influence**: Moderate - Established natural pro pathway, historical significance, dedicated following
- **Key Features**: Professional and amateur divisions, established history, drug-free competition
- **Competition Levels**: Clear advancement pathway from amateur to professional status
- **Testing Protocol**: Comprehensive drug testing at all competition levels

### 11. **World Beauty Fitness & Fashion (WBFF)**
- **Description**: International fitness modeling and physique organization emphasizing aesthetics, stage presence, and overall presentation. Founded with a focus on combining fitness competition with fashion and modeling elements, the WBFF attracts athletes who excel in both physique development and presentation skills. The organization hosts events worldwide and has become known for its high production values and emphasis on complete athlete presentation.
- **Annual Competitions**: 5-10 major events in the USA (part of global circuit)
- **Influence**: Moderate-High - Global fitness modeling leader, aesthetic focus, mainstream appeal
- **Key Features**: Fitness modeling focus, international reach, high production values, emphasis on presentation
- **Divisions**: Fitness model, diva bikini model, muscle model, transformation categories
- **Global Presence**: Events across multiple continents with international championships
- **Website**: [wbffshows.com](https://www.wbffshows.com)

### 12. **Musclemania**
- **Description**: International natural bodybuilding organization with global competitions and model searches. Musclemania combines traditional natural bodybuilding competition with fitness modeling opportunities, hosting events worldwide and providing platforms for athletes to showcase both their physiques and presentation skills on an international stage.
- **Annual Competitions**: 3-8 major events in the USA (part of international series)
- **Influence**: Moderate - International natural brand, model search focus, global recognition
- **Key Features**: International competitions, model searches, global reach, natural standards
- **Competition Format**: Traditional bodybuilding combined with modeling opportunities
- **Global Events**: Competitions held across multiple continents with world championships

### 13. **International Natural Bodybuilding Federation (INBF)**
- **Description**: Natural bodybuilding with international reach and comprehensive drug testing. The INBF provides drug-free competitive opportunities for athletes worldwide, maintaining strict testing protocols and promoting the values of natural bodybuilding across different countries and cultures.
- **Annual Competitions**: 2-5 contests in the USA (primarily international focus)
- **Influence**: Low-Moderate - International scope, limited USA presence, niche following
- **Key Features**: International scope, comprehensive drug testing, natural bodybuilding focus
- **Testing Standards**: Regular drug testing, banned substance monitoring, international compliance
- **Global Network**: Affiliated organizations in multiple countries
